# Usability Testing

## Tested Feature

The feature selected for usability testing is the **“Add Category”** form in the **Toolshop** web application.
This interface allows users to create a new product category by filling out fields including `Parent Id`, `Name`, and `Slug`, and then clicking the **Save** button or the **Back** link to return to the previous screen.

![“Add Category” Page - 2](images/0.png){ height=300px }


## Survey Design

A usability survey consisting of **11 questions** was designed using **Google Forms**.
The questions covered various aspects such as clarity of labels, ease of interaction, layout alignment, and overall user satisfaction.
The survey included **multiple-choice**, **Likert scale (1–5)** ratings, and **one open-ended question**.

*Survey Link:* \[[Google Form link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdOlbz4eHM0V-LWXRk9rCAFjd7-3LJ63H3FVeoxspSRxYaBSQ/viewform?usp=dialog)]

A total of 7 participants completed the survey. The demographic breakdown is as follows:

- 2 high school students

- 4 university students

- 1 working professional

This mix of participants helped ensure that the feedback captured a range of perspectives, from beginner to more experienced users.

##  Summary of Responses

Below is a summary of key insights gathered from user feedback:

* **71.4%** of users had never heard of the “Add Category” feature before
* **85.7%** clearly understood the purpose of the screen
* **100%** found the input fields and **Save** button easy to locate and use
* **28.6%** said the label **"Parent Id"** was unclear
* **42.9%** said the label **"Slug"** was confusing or ambiguous
* **28.6%** rated the form layout and spacing at only 3 out of 5, suggesting room for layout improvement
* Overall satisfaction was moderate, with the majority of users selecting a score of 3 or 4 out of 5, and no users giving the highest rating.

![Overall satisfaction](images/5.png){ height=300px }

## Usability Concerns Reported by Users

Some of the most notable concerns or suggestions raised through open-ended feedback include:

* The label **“Parent Id”** is misleading.
* Lack of tooltips or placeholder text for fields such as **Slug**, causing confusion.
* Footer is too close to the content area and appears visually unbalanced.
* The overall visual design feels minimal or plain; users suggested improving color contrast and repositioning the Save button.

## Conclusion and Recommendations

The **“Add Category”** feature was generally perceived as **usable and easy to understand**, even by first-time users.
However, a few usability concerns emerged, mostly related to ambiguous field labels and UI layout.

**Suggested improvements:**

* Replace technical terms like **Slug** with user-friendly alternatives or add short explanations
* Display clear feedback (success/error) after form submission
* Reposition and resize the **footer** for better balance
* Enhance visual clarity by adjusting spacing, alignment, and action button placement
